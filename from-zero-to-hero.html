FROM ZERO TO HERO

– JAPANESE-THEMED GAME CONCEPT

3RD PLACE

From Zero to Hero is a game concept created for a development competition. It follows a female samurai who fights injustice in a divided town and seeks to remove the wall separating the rich from the poor, restoring dignity, fairness, and social equality.

STORY CORE

The heroine begins with limited power and status, but grows through courage, strategic choices, and service to the community. Her central mission is not simply defeating enemies: it is confronting a social system that keeps people separated.

◉ PROJECT SCOPE

  • Developed as a game concept rather than a playable build.
  • Defined the main character, world, conflict, and message.
  • Explored Japanese-inspired visual and environmental themes.
  • Prepared concept material for competition presentation.

▣ AREAS OF WORK

GAME DESIGNNARRATIVE DESIGN CONCEPT DEVELOPMENTVISUAL DIRECTION CHARACTER DESIGNPRESENTATIONTEAMWORK

⌁ KEY CONCEPTS

  • Female samurai lead character.
  • Japanese-inspired setting and visual identity.
  • Justice, equality, and class division as central themes.
  • A physical wall representing social and economic separation.
  • Growth from an ordinary beginning to a heroic role.

◌ DESIGN CHALLENGE

Challenge: Creating a clear social message while keeping the concept dramatic, visually memorable, and suitable for gameplay.

Approach: Connected the heroine’s growth to the town’s transformation and used the wall as both a story element and symbolic objective.
