MISSION_04 ////

SMART PILL BOTTLE

– HEALTHCARE IoT PROJECT

COMPLETE

An IoT-based medication support system designed to help patients take scheduled doses on time. The prototype monitors the pill compartments and sends real-time Home Assistant notifications when a dose is due or appears to have been missed.

◉ MY ROLE

  • Helped design the prototype architecture and hardware integration.
  • Worked with the microcontroller, sensors, buzzer, and indicator LEDs.
  • Developed reminder, detection, and notification logic.
  • Connected the prototype to Home Assistant for real-time alerts.

▣ TECHNOLOGIES

RASPBERRY PI ARDUINO / ESP32 HOME ASSISTANT SENSORS WI-FI ELECTRONICS IoT

⌁ KEY FEATURES

  • Scheduled medication reminders.
  • Light and buzzer feedback for the patient.
  • Real-time notifications through Home Assistant.
  • Missed-dose alerts for the patient and family.
  • Physical prototype with multiple pill compartments.

◌ CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

Challenge: Connecting physical sensor events to reliable reminders without generating unnecessary alerts.

Solution: Tested the sensor and timing logic, combined visual and audio feedback, and used Home Assistant automation to verify notification delivery.
